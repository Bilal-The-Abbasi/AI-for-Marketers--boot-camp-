Why it works

      
A plan this short does one job: it makes every handoff visible. You can see which steps the AI does, which a person does, and where a person must look before anything ships.

      
Share it before you start. If a teammate cannot tell who checks what, the plan is not done.

Go deeper

      

        Run it, then review it
        

          
Run the plan once on a small slice, such as one email or one week of posts. Note what the AI got wrong and where your checkpoint caught it. Fix the plan, not just the draft.

          
After a month, put the plan next to the numbers from Prove It. Keep what paid off, cut what did not.
