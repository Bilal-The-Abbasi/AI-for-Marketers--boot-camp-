Why it works

Every AI idea has two costs: the effort to set it up and the damage if it goes wrong. The map keeps both in view. Impact is what you gain if it works. Risk is what happens if it quietly fails.

Start in the top left. A quick win teaches your team how to supervise AI while the downside stays small. Big bets are fine later, with a pilot, a cap, and a person watching.

Where a person belongs in the loop

Think of three seats. In the first, the AI drafts and a person decides. In the second, the AI acts and a person reviews a sample. In the third, the AI acts alone. Most marketing work belongs in the first seat for a long time.

Moving a task to the next seat should be earned with evidence: weeks of good results, a cap on the damage, and a clear way to switch it off.
