Why it works

      
The template splits your prompt into two kinds of lines. Fixed lines (role, voice, constraints, example, format, the no-invented-facts rule) stay the same every time. Placeholders in curly braces are the only parts you change.

      
That is the whole kit. Next time you need a campaign email, you paste the template, swap {offer} and {facts}, and run it. Ten seconds, same quality, same voice.


      
Check

      

        
Paste one template into your AI tool, replace the placeholders with real details, run it, then tick whatever is true.

        Keeping the kit alive
        

          
Save the three templates in one note, one heading each. When a result disappoints, fix the template, not just the output. A new line in the template helps every future run.

          
Add a version date to each. When a template stops working, you will know which edit broke it.
