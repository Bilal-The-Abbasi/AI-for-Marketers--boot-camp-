    Why it works

    Remember the odds from Mission 1? Every line you add narrows the field. With no audience, the AI guesses “everyone” and writes for no one. Give it an audience, a goal, some rules, and a voice, and it stops guessing at things you already know.

    Better prompts are mostly better briefs, the same kind you would hand a freelancer on day one. Voice is the hardest to put in words. “Friendly” means almost nothing. One real subject line that worked shows it in a single line.

    Fix a near miss without starting over

    If the output is close but off, do not start a new chat. Tell it what to change: “shorter, drier, drop the second line.” It keeps the context and adjusts, and you spend your effort on the part that is actually wrong.

    When you can, show instead of describe. One example of the result you want usually beats a paragraph of adjectives.
