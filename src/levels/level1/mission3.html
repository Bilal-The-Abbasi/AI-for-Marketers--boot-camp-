Why it works

It ranks likely words. It does not check facts. A statistic with a source attached sounds right because real statistics with sources are common in what it learned from, so it produces one with the right shape and nothing behind it.

Four tells give most of them away. Run this 10-second check on anything you plan to publish.

  • NumbersDid I give it this number?
  • Quotes and namesDid this person really say this?
  • Dates and “latest”Could this have changed since it learned it?
  • Always and neverIs it really that absolute?

How to make it more honest

Paste in your own facts and tell it to use only those. Tell it to ask you, or say “I don’t know,” when something is missing. If your tool has web search, turn it on, then open the sources it cites, because it can still cite the wrong thing.

None of this removes the check. It just makes the pile of things to check smaller.
